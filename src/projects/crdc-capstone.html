Private Industry Capstone · Sanitised Case Study

AI-Assisted Scientific Research Synthesis

CRDC Industry Capstone exploring retrieval-augmented generation and agent workflows for historical scientific research documents. Code, data and research documents are private.

PythonRAGE5 retrievalCross-encoder rerankingLocal Gemma / Ollama

Public Boundary

This case study discusses workflow structure and technical approach only. It does not expose private source code, partner documents or confidential research data.

Problem

The project explores how retrieval-augmented generation and agent workflows can help researchers work across collections of historical scientific reports and produce more structured, traceable evidence synthesis.

Research Workflow

The public-safe workflow separates a portfolio research question from the analytical task and then from the retrieval-only query. That separation makes the retrieval step more explicit and easier to evaluate.

Specialist Agents

Different specialist agents handle different analytical intents instead of giving every agent exactly the same context. Recurring capabilities explored include evidence synthesis, temporal analysis and critical evidence analysis.

Retrieval Pipeline

The team retrieval pipeline includes E5 bi-encoder retrieval and cross-encoder reranking. NotebookLM was used only as an experimental comparison/reference tool, not as ground truth.

My Contribution

  • Python agent/reporting workflow code.
  • Agent/retrieval integration and evaluation workflow.
  • Separating research questions from analytical and retrieval intent.
  • Testing task-specific retrieval.
  • Evaluating evidence at document and chunk level.
  • Experimenting with temporal diversification after ranking.

Public-Safe Experiment

A temporal retrieval experiment used a 15-document biosecurity corpus covering historical material from approximately 1996 to 2017. A revised post-ranking temporal diversification approach selected evidence from five distinct years: 1998, 1999, 2006, 2008 and 2016.

This is presented as an experiment and observation, not as a percentage improvement claim.

What I Learned

Document-level relevance does not guarantee that a retrieved chunk is useful for the analytical task. Different research questions need different retrieval intent instead of one generic retrieval query.
